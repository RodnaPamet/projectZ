## 2. What the service does

playerz.bg connects players, clubs and coaches:

- players book courts at clubs;
- players post open games and join them;
- players record results, follow their level and take part in tournaments;
- players, clubs and coaches write messages to each other;
- clubs and coaches manage their hours and lessons.

**We are not a party to the booking.** The contract for using the court is between you and the club. A booking made online is confirmed at once and paid at the club. There is no online payment on playerz.bg at present.

We do not organise the game itself and we are not responsible for the venue. The club is responsible for the safety of the court and for its rules.

## 4. Bookings, cancellations and no-shows

- **How far ahead.** The app offers today and the next 13 days.
- **How many at once.** You can have up to 3 upcoming online bookings at one club. The club can change that number.
- **Cancelling.** You can cancel a booking in the app until the club's deadline. That is usually 24 hours before the start, but the club can choose another deadline, up to 7 days before the start. After the deadline, only the club can cancel the booking.
- **No-shows.** If you do not show up three times within 90 days at one club, your online bookings there are stopped. They stay stopped until fewer than three no-shows fall within the last 90 days, or until the club lets you book again sooner.
- **A new account.** If you delete your account and make a new one with the same email address or phone number, the no-shows move to the new account. Each one counts for 90 days from the day it happened.
- **Credit at a club.** Credit is between you and the club. After you delete your account you can no longer see or use the balance on playerz.bg; the club keeps its record of past movements. Settle any balance with the club before you delete your account.
- **The club decides.** The club sets prices, hours and the venue's rules.

## 5. Games, results and rankings

- Post only games that will really be played.
- Record the true results. A result is recorded or confirmed within 7 days of the match.
- Levels and rankings are worked out from the results. Do not try to fix them with made-up matches.
- You can hide your level under Profile → Privacy.

## 6. Reviews

- Only a player who has played at a venue on a booking can review that venue. Only a player who was on a completed lesson with a coach can review that coach.
- We publish positive and negative reviews in the same way. There are no paid or sponsored reviews.
- Before it is published, the text of every review is checked by an artificial intelligence model from Anthropic (see section 10).

## 7. Coaches

- A lesson is a contract between the coach and the player. We are not a party to it.
- A coach sees a player's phone number only for an upcoming lesson, and may use it only for that lesson.
