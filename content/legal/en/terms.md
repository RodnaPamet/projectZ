## 1. Who you are agreeing with

playerz.bg is run by **Inflect Ltd**, VAT number BG208694569, of [ADDRESS — owner to fill]. How we handle your personal data is described in the [Privacy policy](/privacy).

## 2. What the service does

playerz.bg connects players, clubs and coaches:

- players book courts at clubs;
- players post open games and join them;
- players record results, follow their level and take part in tournaments;
- players, clubs and coaches write messages to each other;
- clubs and coaches manage their hours and lessons.

**We are not a party to the booking.** The contract for the court is between you and the club. A booking made online is confirmed at once and paid at the club. There is no online payment on playerz.bg today.

We do not organise the game itself and we are not responsible for the venue. The club is responsible for the safety of the court and for its rules.

## 3. Your account

- One person has one account. An account is of one kind: player, club or coach. The kind cannot be changed.
- You sign in with a Google account. Keep access to it safe. If you think someone else is using your account, write to us.
- Your details must be true: your name, your sports and the level you give.
- When we ask for a phone number, it must be yours. You confirm it with a code sent by SMS.
- A club account is run by a person who is entitled to act for the club.

## 4. Bookings, cancellations and no-shows

- **How far ahead.** You can book for today and the next 13 days.
- **How many at once.** You can have up to 3 upcoming online bookings at one club. The club can change that number.
- **Cancelling.** You can cancel a booking in the app until the club's deadline. That is usually 24 hours before the start, but the club can choose another deadline, up to 7 days before the start. After the deadline, only the club can cancel the booking.
- **No-shows.** If you do not show up three times within 90 days at one club, your online bookings there are stopped. The club can let you book again. If you delete your account and make a new one with the same email address or phone number, the no-shows stay.
- **Credit at a club.** Credit is between you and the club. If you delete your account, unused credit is lost.
- **The club decides.** The club sets prices, hours and the venue's rules.

## 5. Games, results and rankings

- Post only games that will really be played.
- Record true results. A result is recorded or confirmed within 7 days of the match.
- Levels and rankings are worked out from the results. Do not try to bend them with made-up matches.
- You can hide your level under Profile → Privacy.

## 6. Coaches

- A coach profile is shown to players after we approve it. Before that, we check the certificate you upload.
- A lesson is a contract between the coach and the player. We are not a party to it.
- A coach sees a player's phone number only for an upcoming lesson, and may use it only for that lesson.

## 7. Clubs

- The club is responsible for the accuracy of its hours, prices and rules.
- The club sees data about the players who book with it: their names, email addresses, bookings, no-shows, credit, and the phone number for an upcoming booking. The club may use it only for the bookings and to contact the player.
- A club account is deleted on request.

## 8. What you may not do

- Do not use the service to break the law.
- Do not make bookings you do not mean to use.
- Do not insult or harass other people in messages, reviews or game notes.
- Do not post another person's personal data without a reason.
- Do not pretend to be another person, or a club that is not yours.
- Do not write reviews of venues you have not played at, or of coaches you have not trained with.
- Do not try to reach other people's data. Do not probe the service for weaknesses without asking us first. Do not get around our limits with automation.

## 9. How we moderate, and how you can object

We moderate the parts of the service that other people see: messages, reviews and game notes.

- **Reports.** You can report a message, a conversation, a game note or a review. A person reads the report. For a reported message, they see the message and the last 20 messages of the conversation. They do not see who filed the report.
- **Reviews.** The text of every review is first checked by an artificial intelligence model. Clearly unacceptable text is refused automatically. If in doubt, the review goes to a person.
- **What we can do.** Remove a message, close a conversation, delete a game note or refuse a review. We can suspend or close a club account. In a serious or repeated case, we can also close a player or coach account.
- **If you think we got it wrong**, write to us at [ivo@inflect.bg](mailto:ivo@inflect.bg). A person will look at the decision again. You can also turn to the Commission for Consumer Protection or to the courts. You do not have to come to us first.

The service is still being developed. Features change, some are not finished yet, and there will be interruptions. We aim to keep your data safe and the service running. But we do not promise that it will be available at every moment or that it will have no faults.

We back up the database every night. So under normal conditions the most we would lose is one day's data.
