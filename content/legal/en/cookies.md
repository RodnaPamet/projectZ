## 1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small file a site saves in your browser. On your next visit, the browser sends it back to the site. Much like cookies, a site can also keep a little data in the browser's storage (localStorage).

## 2. What playerz.bg uses

playerz.bg uses **strictly necessary** cookies only. They are needed to sign you in, to keep sign-in secure, and to remember the language and theme you chose. That is why we do not ask for your consent.

We have no cookies for advertising, tracking or analytics. We load no scripts from other sites.

## 3. Cookies

| Name                                                                  | For what                                                                   | How long                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `__Secure-next-auth.session-token` (split into `.0`, `.1` when large) | Keeps you signed in to your account.                                       | 7 days from sign-in. Then you sign in again. |
| `__Host-next-auth.csrf-token`                                         | Protects sign-in.                                                          | Until you close the browser.                 |
| `__Secure-next-auth.callback-url`                                     | Where to take you back after sign-in.                                      | Until you close the browser.                 |
| `__Secure-next-auth.pkce.code_verifier`                               | Protection during sign-in with Google.                                     | 15 minutes.                                  |
| `__Secure-next-auth.state`                                            | Protection during sign-in with Facebook, once Facebook sign-in is offered. | 15 minutes.                                  |
| `NEXT_LOCALE`                                                         | The language you chose.                                                    | 1 year.                                      |
| `playerz_theme`                                                       | The theme (light or dark) you chose.                                       | 1 year.                                      |
| `pz_phone_later`                                                      | Remembers that you pressed "Not now" when we asked for your phone number.  | 30 days.                                     |

`NEXT_LOCALE` and `playerz_theme` are written when you choose a language or theme yourself. While you are signed in, `NEXT_LOCALE` is updated on every request where it differs from your account's language. It then holds your account's language.

## 4. Browser storage (localStorage)

| Name                        | For what                                                |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `playerz:theme`             | The theme you chose.                                    |
| `playerz:sidebar-collapsed` | Whether you collapsed the side menu.                    |
| `playerz:cookie-notice`     | Remembers that you hid the cookie message.              |
| `venues.radiusKm`           | The distance you chose when searching for clubs nearby. |

This data stays in your browser until you delete it.

## 5. The iPhone app

The app does not use cookies for sign-in. At sign-in it gets two access keys from us. The first is valid for 15 minutes. The second is valid for 30 days from sign-in, and its period is not extended. The app uses the second key to get a new one when the first expires.

## 6. Other sites

- Venue and profile photos load from Google Cloud Storage. That service sees your IP address but sets no cookies.
- The clubs map loads its images from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which sees your IP address.
- The "Directions" button opens Google Maps. If you press it, Google's cookie rules apply.

## 7. How to delete cookies

You can delete the cookies and the site's storage in your browser's settings. You will then be signed out, and the language and theme will go back to the default settings.

There is more about your personal data in the [Privacy policy](/privacy). If you have questions, write to us at [ivo@inflect.bg](mailto:ivo@inflect.bg).
