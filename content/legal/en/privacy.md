# Privacy policy

In force from: [DATE — owner to fill]

This policy explains what personal data playerz.bg collects, what we use it for, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have.

playerz.bg is a sports platform. Players book courts, get together for games and follow their level. Clubs and coaches manage their hours.

The data controller is **Inflect Ltd**, VAT number BG208694569, of [ADDRESS — owner to fill].

For any question about your personal data, and for any request under this policy, write to us at [ivo@inflect.bg](mailto:ivo@inflect.bg).

## 2. What data we collect

### Account and sign-in

- **Sign-in.** You sign in with your Google account. Signing in with Facebook or Apple will only be possible once we offer it. From Google we receive your email address, name and picture.
- **What we keep from sign-in.** Your email address, name and language. Of your picture we keep our own copy: a small image with no metadata. We keep no password, and we do not keep the access keys Google gives us at sign-in.
- **Account kind.** Player, club or coach. An account is of one kind only.
- **Sports and level.** The sports you chose and the level you gave yourself (1 to 7). If you fill in the level questionnaire, we keep only the resulting level, not your answers.
- **Settings.** Language, notifications, whether others can find you in search, and whether they see your level.
- **Accepted texts.** Which version of the terms and of this policy you accepted, when, and in which language.
- **Favourite venues**, if you mark any.

### Phone

When we ask for a phone number, you confirm it with a code sent by SMS. Infobip sends and checks the code. We do not keep it.

We keep the number encrypted. We also keep when it was confirmed, and a protected fingerprint of it. The fingerprint stops one number from confirming two accounts. The number cannot be recovered from it.

### Bookings, games and results

- **Bookings.** Club, court, time, price, who made the booking and who plays in it. Also cancellations and their reason, no-shows, and notes on the booking.
- **What the club records about you.** Tags, the number of no-shows, when you last played, and your credit at the club.
- **Open games.** The games you post, the note on them (up to 280 characters), and your requests to join.
- **Results and level.** The results of your matches and the level worked out from them.
- **Tournaments.** The tournaments you are entered in (with your partner), and the results.

### Messages, reviews and reports

- The messages you write to other players, to a club, or in a game's chat.
- Your ratings and reviews of venues and coaches.
- The people you have blocked and the reports you have filed.

### If you are a coach

Your description, qualifications, sports and lessons. Also the certificate you upload for checking. For that, see section 4.

### If you work for a club

The club's details, who works there and in which role, the email addresses of invited staff, and the venue's photos.

### The "For clubs" form

If a club writes to us through the form on the home page, we keep the person's name, the club's name, the phone number or email address, the message and the form's language. We do not keep the IP address.

### Devices and notifications

- **A phone with the app.** The key Apple uses to deliver notifications to it, the device's name and its system version.
- **Notifications.** The notifications we show you in the app.
- **Emails.** The subject and text of the emails we send you.

### Technical data

- **IP address.** We use it to limit requests that come too often and to protect against abuse.
- **Signing in from the iPhone app.** At each sign-in we keep the IP address and the kind of device.
- **Usage counts.** We count how many times a day a page is opened or an action is taken. These counts are not linked to anyone.

## 3. What we use it for, and on what basis

We process your data under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

- **To run the service for you** (contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your account, bookings, games, messages, results, rankings and the notifications about them.
- **Our legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f)). Security and protection against abuse. The no-show rule, including after an account is deleted (see section 12). Checking reviews. Being able to prove which texts you accepted. The weekly fact about your sport, which you can switch off.
- **Your consent** (Article 6(1)(a)). The Google Calendar connection and the assistant, when you switch them on. You can withdraw your consent at any time by switching them off.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We have no advertising networks and no outside analytics.

### Automated decisions

Two things happen automatically:

- **No-shows.** If you do not show up three times within 90 days at one club, your online bookings at that club are stopped. They stay stopped until the club lets you book again.
- **Reviews.** The text of every review is checked by an artificial intelligence model. Clearly unacceptable text is refused. If in doubt, or if the check is not working, the review goes to a person.

If you disagree with such a decision, write to us. A person will look at it.

## 4. Who sees your data

- **All visitors.** If you post an open game, the list of games shows your first name and picture, the note, the venue, the time and the price. Your email address and phone number are not shown.
- **Other users** see your level, unless you have hidden it. Signed-in users see the club rankings with full names, and tournament draws.
- **Search.** If you allow people to find you, others see your name, picture, sports and levels. You can switch this off under Profile → Privacy.
- **Players in one game** see the names of the other players in it. Only the organiser, the players and those who asked to join see the list of players.
- **The club** you book at, belong to, or write to sees:
  - your name and email address;
  - your bookings, no-shows and credit;
  - your messages to the club;
  - your phone number, but only for an upcoming booking;
  - the tags the club has added to you.
- **A coach** you have a lesson with sees your name, your level and your rating (unless you have hidden it). They see your phone number only for an upcoming lesson.
- **A review of a coach** is shown with a short name, for example "Ivan P.".
- **A coach's certificate** is hidden. Neither clubs, nor players, nor the coach after uploading it can see it. Only the people at playerz.bg who approve coaches can open it, and every opening is recorded.
- **Our team.** Access is granted personally to one person, for a limited time (up to 90 days), and every action is recorded. If someone reports a message, the moderator sees that message and the last 20 messages of the conversation. They do not see who filed the report.

## 6. Data from Google

### Sign-in with Google

At sign-in we ask Google only for `openid`, `email` and `profile`. We receive your email address, name and picture. We use them only to create your account and so that you can sign in to it.

### Google Calendar

This feature works only if you switch it on in Profile with the "Sync with Google Calendar" switch. We then ask Google for two permissions:

- `calendar.app.created`. With it we make a separate calendar named **playerz** in your Google account. We write your bookings, games, lessons and tournament matches into it. Each event holds a title, the time, the venue's name and address, the court and a link to playerz.bg. Other players' names are not written. We can neither read nor change your other calendars.
- `calendar.freebusy`. With it we see only when you are busy and when you are free. We do not see titles, places or names.

What we use "free/busy" for: when another player on one of your bookings asks the assistant for a good time for a new game, the assistant checks when you are free. The other player learns only whether you are free at the proposed times. They do not learn what is in your calendar.

We keep the access key to your calendar encrypted. We also keep the email address of the Google account you connected, the permissions granted, and which calendar is the playerz one.

**How to switch it off.** Turn off the switch in Profile. We then remove our access at Google and delete the key. The playerz calendar stays in your Google account, and you can delete it yourself. The same happens if you delete your playerz.bg account.

We do not sell data from Google. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train artificial intelligence models either.

Google's sentence, which we are required to quote word for word:

> playerz.bg's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 7. The assistant

The assistant works only when we switch it on. It is for the accounts that play: players and coaches. The assistant uses an artificial intelligence model from Anthropic. It can help you find a free hour or a good time to play. It books nothing by itself. It only proposes a draft, and you confirm it.

**What we keep:**

- your questions and the answers;
- up to 50 short things the assistant has remembered about you (up to 280 characters each), and for each one the sentence it learned it from;
- how much the assistant cost us for you each day.

**What we send to Anthropic:**

- your question and up to 6 earlier turns of the conversation;
- the things the assistant remembers about you;
- the search results: free hours, clubs, prices, the names of the players on your booking and whether they are free.

Your location is not sent.

**How the assistant remembers.** 10 minutes after a conversation goes quiet, we send your questions to a second Anthropic model. It picks out up to 5 new things to remember.

**Deleting.** You can delete a conversation or a remembered thing. What you deleted then disappears from the assistant and is no longer used. The text itself is erased for good when you delete your account.

## 8. Notifications and emails

- **Notifications on iPhone.** They are sent through Apple. For a message, the notification says only who is writing to you, not the text. Each kind of notification has its own switch in Profile.
- **Fact of the week.** Every Monday from 10:00, players get a fun fact about their sport. You can stop it with its own switch.
- **Emails.** They are sent through Resend. You get them for:
  - booking confirmations and reminders;
  - changes made by the club;
  - open games;
  - new messages;
  - the lessons you teach (if you are a coach).

  The email about a message says only who is writing to you, not the text. We send it no sooner than 10 minutes after the message, and at most once an hour per conversation. Each kind has its own switch, and an unsubscribe link in the email itself. Some account emails, such as the decision on your coach profile, have no switch.
- Emails are plain text, with no hidden tracking images. We send no advertising emails.

## 9. Location and maps

If you let the app use your location to find clubs nearby, we use the coordinates only for that search. We do not keep them.

We draw venue plans on our own server from OpenStreetMap data. We do not load maps from other sites. The "Directions" button opens Google Maps with the venue's address. From there on, Google's rules apply.

Photos on playerz.bg load from Google Cloud Storage. That service sees your IP address but sets no cookies.

## 10. How we protect the data

- Phone numbers, messages and the keys for signing in with a second factor are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are written to the database. Coach certificates and the Google Calendar key are also kept encrypted.
- The email and phone fingerprints are one-way. Neither the email address nor the number can be recovered from them.
- We remove the location and other metadata from uploaded photos.
- The servers, the database and the backups are in the EU.
- Team access is personal and time-limited. Every action is recorded, and changes need a second factor.

## 11. How long we keep the data

- **While you have an account:** your profile, sports, settings, messages, notifications, sent emails, reviews, devices, the Google Calendar connection and the assistant's data.
- **Sessions from the iPhone app**, with the IP address and the kind of device: until you delete your account.
- **Bookings and payments:** they stay with the club after your account is deleted, without your name. The club's accounts are worked out from them. Today we have no set end date for them.
- **No-shows after deletion:** up to 90 days from each no-show (see section 12).
- **A coach's certificate:** while you are an active coach. It is deleted when we stop your coach profile or you delete your account. If a coach profile is a draft or was declined and does not change for 30 days, the certificate is deleted.
- **IP address for protection against abuse:** 2 hours at most.
- **Technical logs:** on our server. The oldest are deleted when the logs pass a set size.
- **The "For clubs" form:** until you ask us to delete it.
- **Accepted texts, and the records of who did what to a booking:** kept after the account is deleted.
- **Backups:** the database copy is kept for 30 days, plus 7 more days in which it can be recovered. The copy of the whole disk is kept for 14 days. Deleted data is gone from the backups no later than 37 days after the deletion.

## 12. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, under Profile → Deleting your account. Deletion is **immediate and final**. There is no period in which you can undo it. There are more details on the page [How to delete your account](/delete-account).

- **When it cannot happen at once.** If you have upcoming bookings, lessons or tournaments, cancel them first or wait until they are over.
- **A club account** is deleted on request. Write to us.

**What is deleted:**

- your name, phone number and picture;
- your email address is replaced, so you can sign up again with the same address;
- your sports, levels, notifications and email settings;
- your reviews;
- your invite links;
- your club memberships and the tags clubs added to you;
- your favourite venues, your blocks and the reports you filed;
- your devices and sessions;
- the text of your messages.

**What stays, and why:**

- **Your bookings** stay with the clubs as "Deleted user", because the club's accounts are worked out from them. Payments and credit movements stay too. Unused credit is lost.
- **Records of when a booking was made or cancelled** stay in the club's history, linked to the deleted account. They do not contain your IP address.
- **No-shows that still count** stay for up to 90 days from each no-show. They are linked to a protected fingerprint of your email address and phone number, not to your name. That way a stop on online booking cannot be dodged with a new account. If you make a new account with the same email address or phone number, the no-shows move to it.
- **The record of which texts you accepted** stays, as proof.
- **Match and tournament results** stay, without your name.
- **Enquiries from the "For clubs" form** are deleted on request.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove playerz.bg's access in your Facebook settings. That does not delete your playerz.bg account.

**The file contains:**

- your profile, phone number and accepted texts;
- your sessions and security events (with the IP address and device);

- your bookings, credit and no-shows;

**The file does not contain:**

- other people's contact details;
- the tags clubs added to you.

Each kind of record in the file holds 10,000 rows at most. If you have more, write to us.

- to correct inaccurate data (you change your name and sports yourself in Profile; your email address comes from Google);
- to ask for erasure;
- to ask for processing to be restricted;
- to get your data in a machine-readable format;
- to object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- to withdraw a consent you gave;
- to have a person review an automated decision that affects you.

Write to us at [ivo@inflect.bg](mailto:ivo@inflect.bg). We will answer within one month.

You have the right to complain to the Bulgarian data protection authority, the Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД): [https://www.cpdp.bg](https://www.cpdp.bg).
