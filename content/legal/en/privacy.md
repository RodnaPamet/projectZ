playerz.bg is a sports platform. Players book courts, get together for games and follow their level. Clubs and coaches manage their hours.

## 2. What data we collect

### Account and sign-in

- **Sign-in.** You sign in with your Google account. Signing in with Facebook or Apple will only be possible once we offer it. From Google we receive your email address, name and picture.
- **What we keep from sign-in.** Your email address, name and language. Of your picture we keep our own copy: a small image with no metadata. We keep neither a password nor the access keys Google gives us at sign-in.
- **Account kind.** Player, club or coach. An account is of one kind only.
- **Sports and level.** The sports you chose and the level you gave yourself (1 to 7). If you fill in the level questionnaire, we keep only the resulting level, not your answers.
- **Settings.** Language, notifications, whether others can find you in search, and whether they see your level.
- **Accepted texts.** Which version of the terms and of this policy you accepted, when, and in which language.
- **Favourite venues**, if you mark any.

### Phone

When we ask for a phone number, you confirm it with a code sent by SMS. Infobip sends and checks the code. We do not keep it.

We keep the number encrypted. We also keep when it was confirmed, and a protected fingerprint of it. The fingerprint stops one number from confirming two accounts. The number cannot be read back from it, but we can recognise the same number if it is used again.

### Bookings, games and results

- **Bookings.** Club, court, time, price, who made the booking and who plays in it. We also keep cancellations and their reasons, no-shows, and notes on the booking.
- **What the club records about you.** Tags, the number of no-shows, when you last played, and your credit at the club. Club staff can make a booking in your name, with a name and phone number they type in themselves. They can also find you by a phone number: we compare it with the fingerprint of your confirmed number. The club can also write notes on your bookings.
- **Open games.** The games you post, the note on them (up to 280 characters), and your requests to join.
- **Results and level.** The results of your matches and the level worked out from them.
- **Tournaments.** The tournaments you are entered in (with your partner), and the results.

### Messages, reviews and reports

- The messages you write to other players, to a club, or in a game's chat.
- Your ratings and reviews of venues and coaches.
- The people you have blocked and the reports you have filed.

### If you are a coach

Your description, qualifications, sports and lessons. We also keep the certificate you upload for checking (see section 6). Before you upload it, please black out your personal ID number (ЕГН) on it.

### If you work for a club

The club's details, who works there and in which role, the email addresses of invited staff, and the venue's photos.

### The "For clubs" form

If a club writes to us through the form on the home page, we keep the person's name, the club's name, the phone number or email address, the message and the form's language. We do not keep the IP address. Each enquiry is also emailed to us through Resend, and a copy of that email stays in our email queue.

### Devices and notifications

- **A phone with the app.** We keep the key Apple uses to deliver notifications to it, the device's name and its system version.
- **Notifications.** The notifications we show you in the app.
- **Emails.** The subject and text of the emails we send you.

### Technical data

- **IP address.** We use it to limit requests that come too often and to protect against abuse.
- **Signing in from the iPhone app.** At each sign-in we keep the IP address and the full device and browser description (User-Agent).
- **Server logs.** For every request to the server we log the page address, the account number and the club number. If a request is stopped for too many attempts, we also log the IP address.
- **Team sign-in with a second factor.** Only playerz.bg staff can turn on a second factor. Each attempt is recorded with the IP address and the device.
- **Usage statistics.** We count how many times a day each page is opened and each action is taken. These numbers are not linked to any particular person.

## 3. Where we get data that does not come from you

- **Google:** your name, email address and picture at sign-in. If you connect Google Calendar, we also receive when you are free.
- **Clubs:** tags, no-shows, credit, bookings their staff make in your name, and staff invitations.
- **Other players:** when they add you to a booking, game or tournament, record a result with you, file a report, or write a review of a coach.
- **Coaches:** the lessons you were on.

## 4. Which data is required

- Your email address and name (from Google) are needed to have an account.
- A confirmed phone number is needed only where we ask for it (for example to book online, when that step is on).
- Everything else is optional: picture, level, search visibility, Google Calendar and the assistant.

## 5. What we use it for, and on what basis

We process your data under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

**Performance of a contract** (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR):

- your account, bookings, games, messages, results, rankings and the notifications about them;
- the open game you post, and showing it to everyone;
- the assistant's answers to the questions you ask it;
- checking a coach's certificate (together with our legitimate interest).

**Steps before entering a contract** (Article 6(1)(b)): enquiries from the "For clubs" form.

**Our legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f)):

- security and protection against abuse;
- the phone check and its fingerprint, and the IP address and device at sign-in from the iPhone app: this protects accounts and the rule "one person, one account";
- the no-show rule, including after an account is deleted (see section 14);
- checking reviews;
- proof of which texts you accepted;
- the weekly fact about your sport, which you can switch off;
- the assistant's memory, so that it helps you better (see section 9);
- the data we keep after an account is deleted: to prove that we followed the rules, to defend legal claims, and for the club's own records.

**Your consent** (Article 6(1)(a)):

- the Google Calendar connection, when you switch it on;
- your location for finding clubs nearby, when you allow it on your device. We do not keep it.

**Legal obligation** (Article 6(1)(c)): when a law requires us to keep data or hand it to an authority.

You can withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect processing before the withdrawal.

**Right to object.** You can object to any processing based on our legitimate interest (Article 21 GDPR). We then stop, unless we have compelling grounds to continue or need the data for a legal claim.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We have neither advertising networks nor outside analytics.

### Automated decisions

Two decisions are made automatically:

- **No-shows.** If you do not show up three times within 90 days at one club, your online bookings at that club are stopped. They stay stopped until fewer than three no-shows fall within the last 90 days, or until the club lets you book again sooner.
- **Reviews.** Before it is published, the text of every review is checked by an artificial intelligence model from Anthropic. Only clear cases are refused automatically: sexual content involving minors and instructions for self-harm. Anything else in doubt goes to a person. Every automatic refusal also opens a case for a moderator.

If you disagree with such a decision, write to us. A person from our team will look at it.

## 6. Who sees your data

- **All visitors.** If you post an open game, the list of games shows your first name and picture, the note, the venue, the time and the price. Your email address and phone number are not shown.
- **Other users** see your level, unless you have hidden it. Showing your level is on by default. Signed-in users see the club rankings with full names, and tournament draws.
- **Search.** Search visibility is on by default. Others then see your name, picture, sports and levels. You can stop this under Profile → Privacy. People you have played with can still write to you then.
- **Players in one game** see the names of the other players in it. Only the organiser, the players and those who asked to join see the list of players.
- **The club** you book at or belong to sees:
  - your name and email address;
  - your bookings, no-shows and credit;
  - your messages to the club;
  - your phone number, but only for an upcoming booking;
  - the tags and notes the club has added.
- **A club you have only written to** sees your name and your messages.
- **The clubs' role.** Each club is a separate controller for the data it records about its own customers: tags, no-shows, bookings made by its staff, and credit. We process that data to provide the service.
- **A coach** you have a lesson with sees your name, your level and your rating (unless you have hidden it). They see your phone number only for an upcoming lesson.
- **A review of a coach** is shown with a shortened name, for example "Ivan P.".
- **A coach's certificate** is hidden. Neither clubs, nor players, nor the coach after uploading it can see it. Only the people at playerz.bg who approve coaches can open it, and every opening is recorded.
- **Our team.** In-app access is granted personally to one person, for a limited time (up to 90 days), and every action is recorded. If someone reports a message, the moderator sees that message and the last 20 messages of the conversation. They do not see who filed the report. The platform's operator also has direct access to the server and the database, to maintain them. They use it only when needed, but this access is not recorded in the app.
- **Public authorities**, when the law requires it.

## 8. Data from Google

### Sign-in with Google

At sign-in we ask Google only for `openid`, `email` and `profile`. We receive your email address, name and picture. We use them only to create your account and so that you can sign in to it.

### Google Calendar

This feature works only if you switch it on in Profile with the "Sync with Google Calendar" switch. Before you connect the calendar, the app explains what we will receive. We then ask Google for two permissions:

- `calendar.app.created`. With it we make a separate calendar named **playerz** in your Google account. We write your bookings, games, lessons and tournament matches into it. Each event holds a title, the time, the venue's name and address, the court and a link to playerz.bg. Other players' names are not written. We can neither read nor change your other calendars.
- `calendar.freebusy`. With it we see only when you are busy and when you are free. We do not see titles, places or names.

**What we use "free/busy" for.** When a player who shares a booking with you asks the assistant for a good time for a new game, the assistant checks when you are free. That player learns:

- whether you are free at the times checked;
- whether you have connected your calendar at all;
- that "busy" also includes your other playerz.bg bookings, at any club.

They do not learn what is in your calendar. To prepare the answer, the assistant sends Anthropic, our AI provider, the name on your profile and only whether you are free at the times checked. Anthropic processes this data for us under contract and may not use it for anything else.

We keep the access key to your calendar encrypted. We also keep the email address of the Google account you connected, the permissions granted, and which calendar is the playerz one.

**How to switch it off.** Turn off the switch in Profile. We then remove our access at Google and delete the key. The playerz calendar stays in your Google account, and you can delete it yourself. The same happens if you delete your playerz.bg account.

We do not sell data from Google and do not use it for advertising. We do not use Google user data to create, train or improve any AI or machine-learning model.

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the [Google User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/workspace/workspace-api-user-data-developer-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 9. The assistant

The assistant is an artificial intelligence program, not a person. It runs on models from Anthropic. It is for player and coach accounts. It is used when you ask it a question. It can help you find a free hour or a good time to play. It books nothing by itself. It only proposes a draft, and you confirm it.

Do not share health details with the assistant.

**What we keep:**

- your questions and the answers;
- up to 50 short things the assistant has remembered about you (up to 280 characters each), and for each one the sentence it learned it from;
- how much the assistant cost us for you each day.

**What we send to Anthropic:**

- your question and up to 5 earlier messages from the conversation (6,000 characters in all at most);
- the things the assistant remembers about you;
- up to 8 excerpts from past conversations, when the answer needs them;
- which screen you have open in the app, and the ids of the things on it;
- your recent bookings;
- the search results: free hours, clubs, prices, distances to the clubs, the names of the players on your booking, and whether they are free.

We do not send your coordinates. Distances to clubs can, however, show roughly where you are.

**How the assistant remembers.** Ten minutes after a conversation stops, we send your questions to another Anthropic model. It picks up to 5 new things to remember.

**What you can do.** You can see, change and delete every remembered thing. You can also delete a conversation. You can object to the assistant's memory (see section 5).

**What happens when you delete.** A deleted conversation disappears from the assistant at once. Its text stays in the database until you delete your account. A deleted remembered thing also stays until you delete your account. We send it to the memory model, but with a note saying "do not learn this again". Deleted things also show in the file with a copy of your data.

  - booking confirmations and reminders;
  - changes made by the club;
  - open games;
  - new messages;
  - the lessons you teach (if you are a coach).

  The email about a message shows only who is writing to you, not the text. We send it no sooner than 10 minutes after the message, and at most once an hour per conversation. The email itself also has an unsubscribe link.

- Emails are plain text, with no hidden tracking images. We send no advertising emails.

## 11. Location and maps

- **In the iPhone app.** If you let the app use your location to find clubs nearby, we use the coordinates only for that search. We do not keep them.
- **On the website.** The clubs map loads its images from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). OpenFreeMap sees your IP address. If you let your browser share your location, we round it to about 100 m, use it only for the search and do not keep it. The distance you chose is remembered in your browser (see the [Cookie policy](/cookies)).
- **Venue plans** are drawn on our own server from OpenStreetMap data.
- **The "Directions" button** opens Google Maps with the venue's address. From there on, Google's rules apply.

## 12. How we protect the data

- Your confirmed phone number, messages and the keys for signing in with a second factor are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are written to the database. Coach certificates and the Google Calendar key are also kept encrypted.
- A name and phone number that club staff type in for a booking, and the phone number and email address from the "For clubs" form, are not encrypted. Database access rules protect them.
- The email and phone fingerprints cannot be read back. But we can recognise the same email address or number if it is used again.
- We remove the location and other metadata from uploaded photos. A coach's certificate is kept as it was sent: encrypted, but with its metadata.
- The servers, the database and the backups are in the EU.
- Team access in the app is personal and time-limited. Every action is recorded, and changes need a second factor.

## 13. How long we keep the data

- **While you have an account:** your profile, sports, settings, messages, notifications, sent emails, reviews, devices, the Google Calendar connection and the assistant's data.
- **Session records:** up to 90 days after the session ends. The sign-in keys themselves expire sooner: 7 days on the website and 30 days in the iPhone app.
- **Bookings, club payment entries and booking history:** 5 years after the booking (Article 110 of the Bulgarian Obligations and Contracts Act). We then delete or anonymise them.
- **No-shows after deletion:** up to 90 days from each no-show (see section 14).
- **Accepted texts after an account is deleted:** 5 years.
- **A coach's certificate:** while you are an active coach. It is deleted when we stop your coach profile or you delete your account. If a coach profile is a draft or was declined and has not changed for 30 days, the certificate is deleted.
- **IP address for limiting requests:** 2 hours at most in the server's memory. It may remain in the server's backups for up to 14 days.
- **Server logs:** the oldest entries are deleted when the logs pass 30 MB per service. There is no fixed number of days.
- **Enquiries from the "For clubs" form:** 2 years after the last contact, or sooner if you ask us to delete them.
- **Backups:** the database copy is kept for 30 days, plus 7 more days in which it can be recovered. The copy of the whole disk is kept for 14 days. Deleted data is gone from the backups no later than 37 days after the deletion.

## 14. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, under Profile → Deleting your account. Deletion is **immediate and final**. There is no period in which you can undo it. There are more details on the page [How to delete your account](/delete-account).

- **When it cannot happen at once.** If you have upcoming bookings, lessons or tournaments, cancel them first or wait until they are over.
- **A club account** is deleted on request. Write to us.

**What is deleted:**

- your name, phone number and picture;
- your email address is replaced, so you can sign up again with the same address;
- your sports, levels, notifications and email settings;
- your reviews;
- your invite links;
- your club memberships and the tags clubs added to you;
- your favourite venues, your blocks and the reports you filed;
- your devices and sessions;
- the text of your messages;
- the Google Calendar connection (we also remove our access at Google);
- your conversations with the assistant and the remembered things.

**What stays, and why:**

- **Your bookings** stay with the clubs under the name "Deleted user", because the club's reports are prepared from them. Payments and credit movements stay too.
- **Credit at a club** is between you and the club. Deleting your account removes it from playerz.bg. Settle any balance with the club before you delete your account.
- **The history of changes** to bookings and staff at the club stays, linked to the deleted account. It can include the name club staff typed in for a booking and the address a staff invitation went to. It does not contain your IP address.
- **No-shows that still count** stay for up to 90 days from each no-show. They are linked to a protected fingerprint of your email address and phone number, not to your name. That way a stop on online booking cannot be dodged with a new account. If you make a new account with the same email address or phone number, the no-shows move to it.
- **The record of which texts you accepted** stays, as proof.
- **Match and tournament results** stay, without your name.
- **Notifications and emails already sent to other people** that name you (for example "New message from Ivan") stay in their accounts.
- **Team sign-in attempts with a second factor** stay recorded, but without the IP address and the device.
- **Enquiries from the "For clubs" form** are deleted on request.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove playerz.bg's access in your Facebook settings. That does not delete your playerz.bg account.

## 16. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right:

- of access: to get a copy of your data;
- to rectification: to correct inaccurate data (you change your name and sports yourself in Profile; your email address comes from Google);
- to erasure;
- to restriction of processing;
- to data portability: to get your data in a machine-readable format;
- to object to processing based on legitimate interest (see section 5);
- to withdraw a consent you gave;
- to have a person review an automated decision that affects you.

Write to us at [ivo@inflect.bg](mailto:ivo@inflect.bg). We will answer within one month. For complex requests this can be extended by two more months. We will then tell you why.

You have the right to complain to the Bulgarian supervisory authority, the Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД): [https://www.cpdp.bg](https://www.cpdp.bg).
